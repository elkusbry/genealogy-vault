# Heinrich Riverstone

**Born:** April 12, 1885 in Bremen, Germany
**Died:** November 3, 1962 in Philadelphia, PA
**Branch:** Heinrich (root)
**Generation:** 1

## Family

**Spouse:** [[Klara Adler (1890)]] (m. June 22, 1909, Berlin)

### Children

- [[Mary Riverstone (1915)]]
- [[Friedrich Riverstone (1918)]]
- [[Else Riverstone (1922)]]

## Parents

- Father: [[Jakob Riverstone]] (cabinet maker in Bremen; details sparse)
- Mother: [[Anna Riverstone]] (maiden name unknown)

## Life Events

- **1885-04-12** — Born in Bremen, Germany. ^[[[1885-heinrich-riverstone-birth-record]]]
- **1909-06-22** — Married Klara Adler in Berlin.
- **1912** — Immigrated to New York with Klara and infant son
  (subsequently lost — see Klara's Stories & Memories). Settled in
  Philadelphia. ^[[[1920-us-census-riverstone-household-philadelphia]]]
- **1937** — Wrote letter to Klara from Vienna while visiting his ailing
  brother; never went back to Germany after this trip.
  ^[[[1937-letter-from-heinrich-to-klara-vienna]]]
- **1962-11-03** — Died of heart failure in Philadelphia.

## Residences

- Bremen, Germany (1885–1912)
- Philadelphia, PA (1912–1962)

## Education & Career

Apprenticed as a cabinet maker in Bremen; opened **Riverstone Furniture
& Cabinetry** at 1837 N 5th Street, Philadelphia in 1915. The shop
operated until 1955.

## Health

Family history of cardiovascular disease (Heinrich's brother also died of
heart failure, c. 1937).

## Death & Burial

Buried at Mount Moriah Cemetery, Philadelphia, alongside Klara (after her
death in 1971).

### Headstone

(Shared headstone with Klara; see [[heinrich-riverstone-tombstone]].)

## Research Notes

**Birth year discrepancy.** Heinrich's German passport (issued 1911) lists
April 12, 1885. The 1912 ship manifest from the *Kronprinzessin Cecilie*
lists "Heinrich Riverstone, age 26" — implying 1886. We default to 1885
(passport is primary); the manifest age was likely a clerk's rounding.
