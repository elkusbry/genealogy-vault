# Klara Adler

**Born:** September 15, 1890 in Berlin, Germany
**Died:** March 21, 1971 in Philadelphia, PA
**Branch:** Heinrich (married in)
**Generation:** 1

## Family

**Spouse:** [[Heinrich Riverstone (1885)]] (m. 1909)

### Children

- [[Mary Riverstone (1915)]]
- [[Friedrich Riverstone (1918)]]
- [[Else Riverstone (1922)]]

## Parents

- Father: [[Aaron Adler]] (uncertain — see Research Notes)
- Mother: [[Hannah Adler]] (uncertain)

## Life Events

- **1890-09-15** — Born in Berlin.
- **1909-06-22** — Married Heinrich Riverstone in Berlin.
- **1912** — Immigrated with Heinrich. Their first child (a son, name
  unknown to family) died at sea on the voyage. Klara never spoke of him.
  ^[[[1912-klara-passport-photo]]]
- **1915** — Mary born in Philadelphia.
- **1937** — Received the Vienna letter from Heinrich.
  ^[[[1937-letter-from-heinrich-to-klara-vienna]]]
- **1971-03-21** — Died of pneumonia.

## Residences

- Berlin (1890–1909)
- Bremen (1909–1912)
- Philadelphia (1912–1971)

## Education & Career

Berlin high school; trained as a bookkeeper. Kept the books for
**Riverstone Furniture & Cabinetry** from 1920 until the shop closed in
1955.

## Death & Burial

Mount Moriah Cemetery, Philadelphia, shared plot with Heinrich.

## Sources

See `sources:` frontmatter.

## Stories & Memories

![[undated-riverstone-family-portrait.jpg]]

Klara was the practical one. She handled the books for the furniture
shop and was known for her speed at arithmetic — Mary recalls her doing
two pages of invoices in the time it took anyone else to do one.

## Research Notes

**Parents uncertain.** Family lore names Aaron and Hannah Adler as
Klara's parents, but no documents have been located. Berlin civil
registers from this period are spotty due to wartime archive losses.

**Lost son on the 1912 voyage.** Multiple family sources mention an
infant son who died on the *Kronprinzessin Cecilie* crossing in 1912.
Ship manifests of that vessel for early 1912 list "Riverstone" with one
adult male, one adult female, and one infant; manifests of subsequent
weeks list only the two adults. Name unrecoverable. See `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
