# Friedrich Riverstone

**Born:** March 27, 1918 in Philadelphia, PA
**Died:** August 9, 1944 near Saint-Lô, Normandy, France
**Branch:** Friedrich (no descendants)
**Generation:** 2

## Parents

## Life Events

- **1918-03-27** — Born in Philadelphia.
- **1942** — Drafted, US Army, 29th Infantry Division.
- **1944-06-07** — Landed at Omaha Beach (D+1).
- **1944-08-09** — Killed in action by German artillery near Saint-Lô.
  ^[[[1944-friedrich-riverstone-handwritten-fragment]]]
- **1948-09-12** — Reinterred at Mount Moriah Cemetery, Philadelphia.

## Education & Career

Apprenticed at Heinrich's furniture shop after high school; no further
education before being drafted.

## Death & Burial

Initially buried at the American military cemetery in Normandy; family
requested reinterment in 1948. Now at Mount Moriah Cemetery in
Philadelphia, near his parents (who joined him there in 1962 and 1971).

## Stories & Memories

> "Freddy was the quiet one. He laughed only at his own jokes, and
> usually only afterward."
— [[Else Riverstone (1922)]], in conversation with [[Linda Sandstrom (1950)]], c. 1985
