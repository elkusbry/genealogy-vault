## Education & Career

Architect at Brennan & Walsh, a firm he co-founded in 1946. Designed
public libraries across New England including the Newton Free Library
(1955) and the Brookline Public Library renovation (1962).

parents nearby. Boston birth records for Feb 14, 1912 list two boys

first-generation Irish-American; "Brennan" is overwhelmingly Irish.
