## Stories & Memories

Mary was the family's keeper of stories. She wrote down everything Klara
remembered about Germany, and most of what's in this vault about
pre-1912 events traces back to her notebooks.

## Research Notes

**Marriage date discrepancy.** The civil record at Suffolk County
(Boston) lists September 11, 1937. The First Lutheran Church register
lists September 18, 1937. Mary and Thomas told their daughter Catherine
they were married "on a Saturday" — September 11 was a Saturday in 1937.
The civil record is primary.
