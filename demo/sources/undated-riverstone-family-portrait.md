# Undated Riverstone family portrait

## Description

Studio portrait of the full Riverstone household: Heinrich and Klara
standing, three children seated in front. Black-and-white silver
gelatin print, oval mat, no photographer's mark visible. Print is in
good condition with minor foxing along the right edge.

## Caption

(None — there's no caption on the original print or back.)

## Extracted Facts

- The portrait must postdate Else's birth (1922) and predate Mary
  leaving for Bryn Mawr (1933). Mary appears to be around 12–14 in the
  portrait — narrowing to roughly **1927–1929**.
- Heinrich wears the same dark suit visible in the 1937 Vienna trip
  photos (different vault, family lore) — consistent with the late-1920s
  estimate.
- All five household members present and apparently healthy. Friedrich
  is the only one not smiling.
