This is the authoritative source for Mary's vital data (birth date,
death date, parents' names) and confirms Heinrich's Anglicized "Henry"
and Klara's Anglicized "Clara" — both rendered here in their
preferred English forms even on an official document, decades after
their own deaths.

The certificate's "place of birth" (Philadelphia) and Mary's parents'
"born Germany" together corroborate the 1920 census data.
