# 1937 marriage record — Mary Riverstone & Thomas Brennan

## Description

Suffolk County, Massachusetts civil marriage register entry,
September 11, 1937. Volume 412, Page 89, License #14437. Marriage
performed by Justice of the Peace Edward R. Connolly at Boston City
Hall.

## Notes

**Date discrepancy.** A separate **church record** (First Lutheran
Church of Boston) records the marriage as September 18, 1937. The
civil record is primary; the church record likely reflects a later
religious ceremony (common at the time for civil weddings of
mixed-faith couples — Thomas was Catholic, Mary Lutheran by upbringing).

This discrepancy is recorded on Mary's person file
([[Mary Riverstone (1915)]]) under `## Research Notes`.

**Thomas's parents marked "Unknown."** This is the first formal
documentation of Thomas's silence about his family of origin. The blank
was filled in his own hand per the register's marginal note.
