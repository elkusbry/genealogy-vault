# Heinrich Riverstone tombstone (shared with Klara)

## Description

Shared upright granite headstone at Mount Moriah Cemetery, Philadelphia,
Section 12, Lot 47. Inscription added in two phases — Heinrich's in
1962, Klara's added in 1971 after her death.

## Cleaned Text

```
RIVERSTONE

HEINRICH                 KLARA
1885 – 1962              1890 – 1971
"Vater"                  "Mutter"

In Treue verbunden
```

Translation of the German line: "Bound in faithfulness" — a traditional
German epitaph; not a quotation.

## Extracted Facts

- Heinrich's death year: **1962** (confirms birth/death from other
  sources).
- Klara's death year: **1971**.
- Heinrich preferred his birth-name spelling on the stone, not the
  Anglicized "Henry" he used in daily life.
- The German inscriptions suggest the family asked for them rather
  than the cemetery's defaults — consistent with their bilingual
  household.
