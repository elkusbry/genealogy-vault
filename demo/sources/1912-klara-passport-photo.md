# 1912 Klara Riverstone passport photo (immigration)

## Description

Sepia-toned head-and-shoulders portrait, oval crop. Mounted on board
stamped "Atelier Reichenbach, Bremen" on the verso. Used for Klara's
1912 emigration papers. The photo is small (approx. 6 × 9 cm) with
visible cracking along the lower mat edge.

## Caption

(None on front. Verso reads simply: "Klara, vor der Reise — April 1912"
— "Klara, before the journey, April 1912" — in Heinrich's hand.)

## Extracted Facts

- Klara was photographed in Bremen in **April 1912**, shortly before
  the family's emigration that summer.
- She would have been 21 at the time.
- The Atelier Reichenbach was a well-known Bremen photography studio
  active 1885–1925, primarily serving emigrant traffic; many German-
  American family albums contain Reichenbach prints from this period.

## Notes

This image is the earliest known photograph of Klara in the vault.
A photographic match against the 1920 US Census enumeration card
(which carries no portrait but a clear physical description) would
confirm the identity — currently `confidence: probable` because the
caption is in Heinrich's hand but the photo carries no name.
